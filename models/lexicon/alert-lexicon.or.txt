# Odia domain lexicon — tasks W4.13 and W7.1.
#
# Distress and operational vocabulary, boosted at weight 1.5 during decoding — see
# docs/ASR.md section 3.4. Negation lives in negation.or.txt at a much higher weight; do
# not duplicate it here.
#
# Odia has the smallest published speech corpus of the ten languages, which makes biasing
# worth more here than anywhere else: the decoder has the least evidence of its own to
# fall back on, so a supplied phrase list moves the result further.
#
# Drafted by an engineer; not yet checked by a native speaker.
#
# One phrase per line, `#` comments, optional weight after a tab.
